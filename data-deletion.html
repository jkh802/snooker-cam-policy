Privacy policyData deletion

These instructions apply to Snooker Cam / Snooker Live, operated by Joel Harris.

Request deletion

Email jkh802@hotmail.com with the subject Snooker Cam data deletion. Describe what you want removed: your Facebook connection, player name/match details, recordings or support correspondence. Include the relevant camera/club, approximate match date and, if needed, your Facebook profile link so the right data can be identified.

Do not send Facebook passwords, access tokens, Wi-Fi passwords or Bluetooth setup keys. We may ask for information needed to verify that you are authorised to request removal. Joel Harris will identify the relevant devices and confirm what was removed or explain any practical limits, such as copies held by someone else.

Remove local recordings yourself

If you are an authorised camera operator, open Videos in the app, select the correct Pi and use the individual delete option or Delete all saved videos on this Pi. Confirm the selection. Active recordings and files in use must be stopped or released first. Deletion removes the selected Pi's recording, associated metadata and prepared export copy. Delete downloaded phone copies and any backups separately.

Revoke Facebook access

In your Facebook account's settings, open Apps and websites (or the equivalent connected-app controls shown by Facebook) and remove Snooker Cam. This revokes the app's authorisation but does not by itself erase its saved Pi files or posts already published on Facebook. Email the contact above to request removal of the Pi's saved Facebook profile details, access token and session records. Device administrators can perform that removal locally.

Clear checked Facebook attempt in the app only clears tracking of a broadcast attempt; it is not an account-data deletion or disconnect control.

Other copies and services

Use Facebook or YouTube's own controls to delete posts or videos hosted there. Copies that other people downloaded or shared may need to be requested from those people. Clearing the Android app's storage removes its local settings and cache but does not erase Pi recordings, Pi account settings, backups or online posts.
