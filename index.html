This policy explains how the Snooker Cam project, including the Android app labelled Snooker Live and its Raspberry Pi software, handles information. Joel Harris operates the project and can be contacted at jkh802@hotmail.com.

What the app does

The app controls a Raspberry Pi camera for snooker previews, local match recordings, score overlays and operator-initiated broadcasts. It uses local Wi-Fi/HTTPS or authenticated, encrypted Bluetooth commands. It does not require a central Snooker Cam cloud account.

Information handled

  • Camera content: previews and recordings may show players, spectators, locations, score overlays and anything else within the camera view. Audio is included only if supported by the installed recording setup.
  • Match details: player names, points, frame results and score-update timestamps entered by operators.
  • Facebook connection: the connected profile's identifier and name, granted permission information checked during connection, and its access token. Broadcast records include identifiers, titles, requested audience, links and status. The current integration uses an access token supplied by the operator; it does not request a Facebook password.
  • Device and network settings: camera/club names, device identifiers, addresses, pairing credentials, Bluetooth setup keys and Wi-Fi network credentials supplied to connect the Pi. Manually configured streaming destinations include their server addresses and stream keys.
  • Operational information: recording times, durations, file sizes, storage status, errors and technical diagnostic logs.
  • Support messages: information you send to the contact address when requesting help or deletion.

How information is used

Information is used to connect and control the camera, show scores, record matches, operate selected streaming services, manage storage, troubleshoot faults and respond to support requests. The current app has no advertising or analytics SDK, and Joel Harris does not sell information collected through the app.

Storage and access

Recordings, match data, Facebook connection details and operational logs are stored on the selected Pi. The phone stores its configuration and pairing details; saved settings are encrypted using Android Keystore-backed protection. Prepared or downloaded videos may also be stored on the phone or copied elsewhere by users. Wi-Fi credentials are stored by the Pi's network manager and are not returned in the app's network list. Pi files rely on operating-system access controls; these protections do not imply that the entire storage card is encrypted.

People holding a camera's control credentials may access its recordings and settings and use its connected streaming account. In the current shared-Pi design, Facebook authorisation belongs to the Pi, rather than being isolated for each phone user. Only give camera-control access to trusted operators. Local storage may also be accessible to the device administrator.

Sharing and third-party services

When an operator starts a Facebook broadcast, the Pi sends the camera feed and selected broadcast information to Meta. The app checks profile identity and permissions with Meta. Facebook governs the actual audience, access restrictions and retention on its platform; a requested Public audience is not a guarantee of public visibility. Sharing a broadcast link or a saved video requires the operator's action. Android sharing may pass a video or link to Facebook, YouTube or another selected app; a separately configured streaming destination receives content when the operator starts it.

These services handle information under their own terms and policies, including Meta's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy. Deleting a Pi recording does not delete a Facebook/YouTube post or copies held by others. When this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles website requests under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This policy page contains no tracking scripts or contact form.

Retention and deletion

Pi recordings remain until an operator deletes them or the configured automatic storage-cleanup policy removes eligible older files. The current cleanup policy can preserve active recordings, interrupted recordings, the newest completed match and files in use. Configuration, connected-account details and operational records may remain until replaced or removed by the administrator. Phone copies, backups and third-party posts have their own retention and deletion controls. Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request and related follow-up.

See data-deletion instructions to remove recordings, revoke Facebook access or request removal of stored account and match information. No fixed automatic expiry of all app data is promised.

Device permissions

Bluetooth/Nearby Devices permissions are used to find and control the Pi. On older Android versions, Android requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning; the app does not use GPS to track your location. Network access connects to the Pi and selected services. Optional installation permission lets Android present updates for your approval.

People recorded and policy changes

Camera operators should tell players and other people present when recording or broadcasting is taking place and agree an appropriate audience. If you have a concern about footage or a player's name, contact Joel Harris with the match, approximate date and camera/club involved. This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes; the date at the top identifies the current version.
